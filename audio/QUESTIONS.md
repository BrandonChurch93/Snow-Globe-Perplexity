# The listener's questions

The same questions for every take of every sound effect. The listener hears the take after the level chain. The open questions are asked first, in a call that does not show the cue's line. The fit questions are asked in a second call. A voice heard in a sound effect counts only if a second call hears it too. A clip shorter than a quarter of a second is given with half a second of silence before it and one second after it, because the listener describes something else every time when the file is that short (measured on `ui-tick` take 1: 0.075 s gave "a film projector" and "a bass drum", the padded copy gave "a sharp metallic clink").

## Open

Listen to this sound. Answer each question in one or two plain sentences, numbered 1 to 5. If you cannot tell, say "not sure".

1. What do you hear? Describe the sound and its character.
2. How many separate sound events are there?
3. Is there any human voice, spoken word, breath or singing?
4. Is there any music, melody or musical instrument?
5. How does it start and how does it end?

## Fit

Listen to this sound. Answer each question in one or two plain sentences, numbered 1 to 3. If you cannot tell, say "not sure".

1. Does it fit this description: "<the cue's line from kit/01-FILM.md>"? Answer yes or no and say why.
2. What in the sound does not belong to the description?
3. Is there any human voice, spoken word or singing in it?

## Family

Asked when a new take joins two kept ones, with the three played one after another in one file, one second apart.

1. Do these three sounds belong together, as if recorded in the same room with the same kind of objects? Answer yes or no and say why.
2. Which of the three, if any, stands apart, and how?

## When the listener says no file came

About one call in five answers that no audio was provided. That call is asked again, once, and the second answer is the one recorded.
