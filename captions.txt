Summer tree, seventh pass. In every picture the approved is on the left and ours on the right.
Drawn on the branch head with main a55e7ee6 merged, review tier, hd, hero camera unless named.

1-whole-400px-approved-left.jpg
The whole at 400 px, with the air. One small dark green tree over the row of lanterns in both. Ours stands fuller and rounder in the crown; the approved shows more sky between its masses.

2-whole-400px-no-air-approved-left.jpg
The same without the air on ours. A measuring aid, not a judged picture.

3-tree-pair-rest-approved-left.jpg
The tree at one scale, at rest, with the air. Ours: a dark crown in soft lobes with a lobed outline, no level band of sky, one notch at the upper left and one small hole by the fork; gold points in unequal groups on the outlines toward the sun; winding boughs that end in leaf; a stem in a slow S that flares into the grass, warm on its left face beside the large lantern.
Not met: the approved keeps several small holes by the fork and shows its boughs through them; ours shows one. Ours is the fuller crown.

4-tree-pair-rest-no-air-approved-left.jpg
The same pair without the air on ours. The leaf reads as rounded lobes, pale green on top and dark underneath. The lit tops are dimmer than the approved's and the leaf reads more as leaves than as the approved's velvet masses.

5-tree-pair-landed-approved-left.jpg
At the landing. The crown stays whole behind the storm: the same masses as at rest, no torn outline, no leaf let go.

6-trunk-no-air-approved-left.jpg
The trunk at five times, without the air on ours, with a grid in millimetres (heights marked at the left). The stem winds, widens into the grass and carries a warm rim on its left face under the lantern. The grass, which is not this part's, hides the foot below a height of about 3.5 mm, so the flare is seen about 1.5 mm higher than the approved's.

7-macro-pair-approved-left.jpg
The macro camera, with the air. Several winding boughs leave the fork and end in leaf; the long thin one at the right carries two lanterns and ends in the low right lobe. The approved has two heavy boughs and rounded holes of sky between them.
