What is the accepted design and what is fitted to this skill's composer:
  From the package with the level change, unchanged: the card's size and radius, the rows, type size and weight, the check mark, the pointer's entry point and path rule (control's middle, then 16 px under the row's top), the hover and pressed fill of the control, every timing (0.55 travel, 0.12 rest, 0.20 menu in, 0.12 read, 0.35 to the row, 0.12 rest, 0.20 settle, 0.16 menu out, 0.18 pointer out, 0.25 before typing).
  Fitted: the menu sits 3 px under the control's fill as there, which is 8 px higher here (this composer's control row is centred on 436, not 444), and its right edge is in line with the control's fill (987), which is 3 px left of the package's absolute x (790 becomes 787). The control's fill spans this skill's whole selector (sparkle, name, level, chevron) by the package's rule of 12 px beyond the glyph and the chevron. Colours are this skill's tokens of the same role: row tint and hover fill #f3f7f8, pressed fill #ebf2f3, labels and check #495a62, the card's shadow and border as this skill's own menu has them.
