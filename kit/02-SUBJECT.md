# 02 · The globe

What is built. `kit/03-METHOD.md` says how. The approved images show how it looks, and where words here and an approved image disagree, the image wins, except on a settled difference.

## 1. The object

A snow globe of the heavy, expensive kind, standing on a dark polished slab in a dark room, lit like a product photograph.

- The glass: a sphere `globe.sphereDiameterMm` across with a wall `globe.glassThicknessMm` thick. Clear, heavy, with soft curved reflections of the lights around it.
- The liquid: fills the sphere, with one small air bubble at the crown that wobbles when the globe moves. It bends light like water, `globe.liquidIor`, so the miniature looks larger through it than it is, and the room behind the globe appears upside down and small around the miniature's edges.
- The miniature: stands on a round island whose top is `globe.islandLevelMm` above the surface.
- The base: `globe.baseHeightMm` tall and `globe.baseDiameterMm` across. The sphere sits `globe.collarDepthMm` deep in its collar.
- The surface: near black, polished like a piano lid. It mirrors the globe softly and catches the glow from inside it.
- The backdrop: darkness with out-of-focus lights in the season's colour, about `scene.bokehLights` of them at different depths.

Everything about the object except the sphere itself belongs to the season.

## 2. The miniature

The layout never changes. A viewer has to see the same place in four seasons, not four places.

| Thing | Where, seen from the hero camera | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The cottage | Left of centre, turned a little toward the viewer | Stone walls, a pitched roof, one chimney, an arched wooden door facing front right, small windows. About a quarter of the dome's height |
| The tree | Right of centre, the tallest thing | One thick twisting trunk, a broad crown reaching to about three quarters of the dome's height |
| The lamppost | Between the cottage and the tree, a little forward | A small black iron street lamp. Lit in every season |
| The pond | Front and centre, at the island's edge | Reflects whatever is above it |
| The fence | Front left, a short run of posts and rails | Straight in three seasons, crooked in fall |
| The path | From the cottage door down toward the pond | Flat stones |
| The island | The ground everything stands on | A low mound, higher at the back |

- The cottage, the trunk, the lamppost, the pond's outline, the fence line and the path are the same geometry in all four seasons. What changes is what covers them, what grows on them, what hangs from them and how they are lit.
- Every season has light coming from inside the globe. It is the first thing the eye goes to.

## 4. How the particles behave

The particles are what the viewer watches most. They are objects in a liquid, not dots on a screen.

- They hang, drift and fall at many depths. Some pass close to the glass, large and soft. Some are far behind the miniature, small and dim.
- They move with the liquid. When the liquid turns, they turn. When it rests, each kind does what it does: flakes sink slowly, petals and leaves slide and tumble, seeds hang almost still, embers rise, fireflies fly where they like.
- They collide. Nothing passes through the cottage, the tree, the lamppost, the fence, the ground or the glass.
- They come to rest. Snow builds on the roof, the branches, the fence rail and the ground. Petals and leaves gather on the ground and the pond and slide off steep places. Seeds snag. What has come to rest is thrown back into the liquid by the next shake.
- They are lit. A flake near the string lights is warm. A leaf near the fog is green on one side.
- Fireflies and bats are never thrown against anything. In a storm they are swept round with the liquid like everything else.

| Kind | At rest in still liquid | Comes to rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flake` | Sinks slowly with a slight sideways wander | Yes, builds up |
| `glitter` | Sinks slowly, turning, flashing when it faces a light | Yes |
| `petal` | Sinks in a slow rocking slide | Yes, lies flat |
| `mote` | Hangs, barely moving | No |
| `seed` | Hangs, drifting with the faintest current | Snags on what it touches |
| `firefly` | Hovers and wanders on its own, blinking | Never |
| `leaf` | Sinks tumbling, faster than a petal | Yes, lies curled |
| `ember` | Rises, brightens, fades, and is born again near a light | Never |
| `bat` | Circles under the crown of the dome | Never |

## 5. The liquid

- The liquid lags the glass. When the globe starts to turn the liquid stays behind, and when the globe stops the liquid keeps turning and takes about `shake.spinDownSeconds` to come to rest.
- A stroke throws it against the trailing wall. Reversals fold it into turbulence.
- After a shake it settles into one slow vortex around an axis that is close to upright, then dies away.
- The veil is densest where the liquid is most disturbed.
- The air bubble at the crown lags and wobbles the same way.

## 6. The shake

This is the hero of the whole piece. It has to look like a hand picked the globe up and shook it hard, and like the globe weighs what glass, water and a carved base weigh. No hand is shown.

It is one bar long and has three parts. The landing is the first frame after it.

| Part | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift | `shake.liftBeats` | The globe leaves the surface: a short dip as if gripped, then up by `shake.liftMm` and tilted back a few degrees, easing. Its mirror image on the surface pulls away from it and its contact shadow opens. The dressing nearest the base is disturbed. The liquid has not caught up yet |
| Strokes | `shake.strokeBeats` | `shake.strokes` strokes, back and forth along a tilted line, each about `shake.strokeMm` long, with the globe rocking up to `shake.tiltDeg` degrees around two axes. They are not evenly spaced and not equal: a hand speeds up, and each reversal is sharp. The liquid slams from side to side and everything that had come to rest is torn loose. The storm fills the glass |
| Set down | `shake.setDownBeats` | The last stroke carries the globe down. It meets the surface exactly on the landing, with weight: a small overshoot in tilt that dies inside `shake.landSettleSeconds`. The mirror image and the shadow close. The dressing reacts |

### The change

The season changes inside the swap window, from beat `shake.swapWindow.fromBeat` to beat `shake.swapWindow.toBeat` of the shake. Nothing changes season outside it.

- The miniature, the glass dressing and the inner lights change while the storm hides them. At that moment at least `shake.minCoverAtSwap` of the miniature is hidden, read from the `cover` view where a pixel counts as hidden at an opacity of `shake.coverOpacity`.
- The particles do not restart. Each particle of the old season becomes a particle of the new one where it is, with the velocity it has, so the storm never breaks. If the two seasons have different counts, the difference fades in or out across the window.
- The veil changes colour through the window: snow dust to petal mist, and so on.
- The base, the dressing and the backdrop are outside the glass and cannot hide in the storm. They change at the fastest moment of the strokes, under motion blur, inside a short bloom of the new season's light pouring out of the globe. Across those few frames the old base gives way to the new. Then the arrival, below, finishes the job where the viewer can watch it.
- The room light crosses from one season's rig to the next across the window.

### The arrival

What happens on and after the landing is what makes the change feel grown, not swapped. Each season arrives in its own way, starting on the landing frame.

| Season | On the landing | Over the next seconds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winter | A ring of frost bursts outward on the surface from the foot of the base | Frost ferns grow in from the edge of the glass. Icicles lengthen. The snow thins and begins to build on the roof |
| Spring | Petals lying on the surface jump and resettle. The first water runs from the base | Beads form on the glass and begin to slide. The mist rises. The storm of petals thins to a few falling from the tree |
| Summer | Sand jumps off the ledge. The sun flare kindles on the glass | The lanterns come up one by one. The fireflies, scattered by the storm, find their places and begin to blink. The pool of light settles on the surface |
| Fall | Dry leaves skitter across the surface. The crack in the glass spreads to its full length | The faces on the base light one after another. The fog pours down and pools. The bats find the crown of the dome |

### After the landing

- The liquid is still turning. The particles sweep round the miniature in one slow vortex and thin as they fall or drift out.
- `states.landedSeconds` after the landing the globe looks like the season's mid-shake approved image.
- `shake.settleCheckSeconds` after the landing at least `shake.minVisibleAfterSettle` of the miniature can be seen again.
- `states.restSeconds` after the landing the globe looks like the season's at-rest approved image.

### What the shake must never do

- Look like a keyframed wobble: even strokes, soft reversals, a globe with no weight.
- Let the miniature, the base or the dressing be seen changing in a clean frame.
- Let the particles pop, restart or pass through anything.
- Move the camera with the globe.

## 7. The app

The hosted piece. A visitor opens it and the globe is there, in the default season, at rest. It is not a menu with a globe behind it. It is the globe.

What a visitor can do:

- Shake it. A press on the Shake control, the space bar, or shaking a phone that has allowed motion. The globe plays the shake and the next season in `seasons` arrives. After the last comes the first.
- Stir it. A short quick drag across the globe sets the liquid turning. The season does not change.
- Look around it. A slow drag turns the camera around the globe, up to `live.orbitYawDeg` degrees either way and between `live.orbitPitchMinDeg` and `live.orbitPitchMaxDeg` above the surface. It eases back toward the front when let go.
- Go straight to a season. Four small marks, one for each season, with the current one lit. Pressing one plays a shake into that season.
- Turn sound on or off.

The interface:

- A few small controls along the bottom edge: the Shake control, the four season marks and the sound control. Nothing else is on screen. No title, no panel, no instructions over the globe.
- The controls are quiet: thin, low contrast against the dark, never pure black or pure white, in one typeface. They dim when the pointer has been still for a while and return when it moves.
- If nothing has been pressed after `live.hintAfterSeconds`, the Shake control pulses once, softly. It never plays the shake by itself.
- Every control works by keyboard, has a name and shows its focus. With reduced motion set, a press changes the season with a slow dissolve and no shake.
- On a phone the globe fills the width and the controls sit under it within reach of a thumb. The app is judged at three sizes: `formats.hd`, `formats.phone` and `formats.tablet`.
- A menu on the `M` key, hidden from the tab order, is for Brandon: quality, season, state, diagnostic view, and the films in `autopilot`.

Sound in the app:

- Silent at first paint. The first press, tap or key anywhere allows sound to start, and the sound control then mutes and unmutes it. The control shows its state from the start.
- Under each season: its ambience, and its music as a loop of `audio.appLoopBars` bars, both quiet. A shake plays `lift`, the incoming season's `shake` cue and `land` cue, and the music changes on the landing, as in the film.
- All sound together is at most `audio.appMaxMb`, and none of it is fetched before the first picture is complete.
- Where the sound files are missing the app is silent and the sound control is not shown.

How it runs:

- It picks a level of `live.quality` by itself from a short measurement, `live.probeFrames` long, and steps down if frames fall under the level's `minFps`.
- A level changes how many particles are drawn, the pixel ratio, the shadow size and how the surface mirrors the globe. It never changes what the globe is.
- In the app the simulation runs on the app's clock. A shake cannot be started while one is playing.

## 9. Framing, checked

The cameras in `cameras` were set from the approved images: there the glass spans about 39 percent of the frame's width and the globe with its base about 84 percent of its height. Three things have to stay true, and a unit test computes each from the config:

| Camera | What must hold | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero` | The height from the foot of the base to the top of the glass, divided by the height of the frame at the globe's distance, lies between `framing.heroFillMin` and `framing.heroFillMax` | The frame's height is twice `distanceMm` times the tangent of half of `fovDeg` |
| `macro` | Every corner of the frame, at the globe's centre plane, lies within `framing.macroMaxRadiusFraction` of the sphere's radius from the sphere's centre, so no edge of the glass shows in frame 0 | The sphere's centre is `globe.baseHeightMm` less `globe.collarDepthMm` plus half of `globe.sphereDiameterMm` above the surface |
| `base` | The frame at the globe's distance is at least as wide as `globe.baseDiameterMm` | |

If you tune a camera and one of these stops holding, the test fails. Tune inside them.
