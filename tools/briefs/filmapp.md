The film:

- `src/film/cut.ts`: the cut list computed in the page from `film.shots` and either the default grid or `cut/AUDIO-MAP.json` when that file exists (it does not yet: accept its shape from `tools/briefs/audiomap.md` and fit the shots by the rules under "Timing" in `kit/01-FILM.md`). It gives everything `tools/README.md` promises in `cut/CUTLIST.json`: frames at `film.fps`, `shots`, `shakes` (`first`, `liftEnd`, `strokesEnd`, `landing`, `swap`), `landings`, `barLines`, `cameraKeys`, `cues`, `ambience`, `opening`, `loop`. `film()` and `filmState(i)` read the same cut, so it cannot drift from what is rendered.
- `src/film/moves.ts`: the camera as a closed-form function of the frame, never stepped, with the lens (aperture and focus) of each shot. The rules of `kit/01-FILM.md`, all of them: frame 0 is the `macro` camera, already moving, pulling back; the pull back is plain inside `film.hookSeconds`; the edge of the glass is in frame by `film.revealBySeconds`; `hero` is reached by the end of the second bar of `open`; `spring` sinks toward `base` and rises again, `summer` pushes toward `interior` and comes back, `fall` goes down to `low` and comes back; in every hold the camera is back at `hero` one beat before the next shake and never stops completely; in a shake it is locked, with at most a few percent of ease in over the bar that settles back after the landing, focus on the glass; `close` is one unbroken push from `hero` to `macro` that holds its last bar; the last frame's camera is frame 0's camera. Ease with smooth curves that have no visible start or stop.
- `npm run cut` (`tools/cut.mjs`) asks the built page and writes `cut/CUTLIST.json`, `cut/CUES.md` (the same in words, for an editor) and `cut/SHOTS.md` (for every shot: the lens, the move, the start and end framing, the focus), all three from the page so none can drift. `npm run simcache` (`tools/simcache.mjs`) calls `dev.simcache` for `film`, `stills` and `loop` in chunks and can be run again to continue.
- `autopilot` plays film B in real time on the page's clock through the live draw path, repeating; `which=a` plays the opening first; `shot=<id>` plays one shot. It plays the film's sound when the sound index names one, and is silent otherwise.

The app, in `src/app/`:

- `live` is the default mode and what the hosted address opens: the globe in the default season, at rest, or in `?season=`. One sample a frame through the same stage. The first frame a visitor sees is a complete picture: nothing blank, no spinner. Set `document.documentElement.dataset.firstPicture = '1'` once it is on screen, and `dataset.audio` as `tools/README.md` says.
- Implement `AppBridge` exactly as `src/core/bridge.ts` types it, with `subscribe` called on every change of `AppState`.
- Shake: the Shake control, the space bar, or shaking a phone that has allowed motion (`devicemotion`, asking for permission on the first tap where the browser requires it). It plays the shake from `src/shake/table.ts` on the app's clock and the next season in `seasons` arrives on the landing. A shake cannot start while one plays. `goTo(season)` plays a shake straight into that season.
- Stir: a short quick drag across the globe sets the liquid turning without changing the season. Call the simulation's `stir` when it has one (type the simulation you use in `src/app/` as a small interface with an optional `stir(x, y, z)`: the particles agent's engine will have it, the measuring simulation does not).
- Look around: a slow drag turns the camera, up to `live.orbitYawDeg` either way and between `live.orbitPitchMinDeg` and `live.orbitPitchMaxDeg`, and it eases back to the front when let go. Tell a stir from a look by speed and length, and make both feel right with a mouse and with a thumb.
- Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`, live): a press changes the season with a slow dissolve and no shake, the globe still.
- Quality in `src/core/quality.ts`: the levels of `live.quality` (the share of particles, the pixel ratio cap, the shadow size, how the surface mirrors, given to the stage through `setQuality` when it has it); `auto` measures `live.probeFrames` frames and picks; the app steps down a level when frames stay under the level's `minFps`; `?quality=` and `setQuality` fix a level. The simulation advances in fixed steps of `sim.stepHz` on the app's clock, never by frame time, and a slow frame never makes it spiral: cap the steps a frame may run.
- Loading: `defaultSeason` first, the others behind it in the order of `seasons`. A shake into a season that is not ready holds the storm for it for at most `live.loadWaitSeconds`, then lands in the season it left. Stand-ins load at once, so prove this in `tests/app.test.ts` with a loader that is late.
- Sizes: `formats.hd`, `formats.phone` and `formats.tablet`. On an upright screen the globe fills the width and leaves room under it for the controls. The canvas follows the window and the pixel ratio of the level.
