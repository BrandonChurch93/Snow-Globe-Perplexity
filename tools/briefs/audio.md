# audio_tools_builder · branch `agent/tools-audio`

Also read: `kit/03-METHOD.md` section 12; in `kit/01-FILM.md` "The soundtrack" with its four parts; `kit/02-SUBJECT.md` section 7; in `kit/07-QUALITY-BAR.md` "Sound checks" and "The listener"; "Sounds" in `kit/06-ASSETS.md`.

You own: `tools/sound.mjs`, `tools/mix.mjs`, `tools/soundcheck.mjs`, `tools/lib/audio.mjs`, `src/audio/` (all of it), `tests/mix.test.ts`.

The repo is public, so no sound file is ever committed: `.gitignore` already holds them. Your tests make their own tones with `ffmpeg` or in code. You do not call the sound tool: the Builder makes the takes and hands them to your commands.

- `tools/sound.mjs`: `--cue ID --file F` measures a take (length, peak, loudness, silence in front, a spectrum summary) and files it under `out/work/audio/takes/<cue>/take-N.wav` with a record; it refuses a take past `audio.maxTakesPerCue` for that cue. `--answers FILE` attaches the listener's answers to the newest take's record. `--keep [--take N]` runs the chain of section 12 (trim the silence, short fades in and out, a low cut, the level of the cue's class from one table in the tool: ambience -30, interface -26, lift and clink -22, landings and shakes -18 LUFS, until the config holds them) and writes `audio/kept/<id>.wav` and `audio/kept/<id>.json` with the take, the prompt, the settings, the measurements and the answers. `--prompt TEXT` and `--settings JSON` record how the take was made. `--pack` builds what the app ships under `public/audio/`: `index.json` in the shape `AudioIndex` of `src/core/bridge.ts`, one MP3 sprite of the short sounds, and one loop file each for `amb-<season>` and `music-<season>` where they exist, each loop encoded as its last 0.2 s, then the loop, then its first 0.2 s, with `loopStart` and `loopSeconds` in the index, so a decoder's padding never lands on the seam; everything together under `audio.appMaxMb`. `--sheet` draws every kept sound as a waveform with its name and length on one picture, `out/work/audio/sheet.jpg`, logged as a look.
- `tools/mix.mjs`: `--candidate NAME` lays one soundtrack on the cut list and writes `audio/mix/<NAME>.wav`; `--candidate blockout` is effects and ambience only, no music; `--final` writes `audio/final/film-b.wav` and `audio/final/film-a.wav`. It reads `cut/CUTLIST.json`: `cues` (`{cue, frame, at}` with `at` in seconds of film B), `ambience` (`{cue, from, to, fadeIn, fadeOut}`) and `opening.cues` for film A, whose opening length shifts everything after it. A cue with no kept file is skipped and named in the output. Mix the PCM in Node, sample by sample, so the same inputs give the same bytes; use `ffmpeg` only to decode and to measure. Bring the result to `audio.filmLoudnessLufs` with a peak at or under `audio.truePeakDb`. `tests/mix.test.ts` proves the byte equality on tones.
- `src/audio/index.ts` exports `createSoundEngine(opts: SoundEngineOptions): SoundEngine` with the types in `src/core/bridge.ts`. Web Audio. The audio context is created only inside `unlock()`, which the app calls on the first interaction. Nothing is fetched before `opts.afterFirstPicture` resolves. A missing cue is silence, never an error. `setSeason` crossfades the ambience and music loops; `shake(to, barSeconds)` plays the lift, the outgoing shake sound and, timed on the bar, the landing of the season it lands in; `stir(strength)` is a soft clink and swirl scaled by strength; `tick()` and `chime()` are the interface sounds. If Web Audio is missing, fall back to plain audio elements under the same interface. `bytesFetched()` reports what was fetched, for check A4. The module must never be imported in `capture` mode: it is loaded with a dynamic import by the app.
- `tools/soundcheck.mjs`: A1 to A8 as the table words them, printed one line each, never failing the process. The page marks audio start with `document.documentElement.dataset.audio` (`none`, then `started`).
