The kit: "They dim when the pointer has been still for a while and return when it moves." The app ships ten of the kit's twenty sounds; `/root` is making the missing ones (`shake-winter`, `shake-summer`, `shake-fall`, `land-winter`, `land-spring`, `land-summer`, `land-fall`, `amb-winter`, `amb-fall`).
