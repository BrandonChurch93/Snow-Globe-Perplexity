# interface_builder · branch `agent/ui`

Also read: `kit/02-SUBJECT.md` section 7 (every line of it), `kit/03-METHOD.md` sections 14 and 15, "The probe" and "Modes" in `kit/05-ARCHITECTURE.md`, and in `kit/07-QUALITY-BAR.md` "R8 · Interface" and "R6 · Scroll stop". Look at `references/approved/contact-sheet.jpg`: the interface sits over pictures like these.

You own: `src/ui/` (all of it), `src/copy.ts`, `src/probe/` (all of it), `tools/ui-preview/`, `public/fonts/`.

- `src/ui/index.ts` exports `mountInterface(root: HTMLElement, bridge: AppBridge): InterfaceHandle`, and `src/probe/index.ts` exports `mountProbe(root: HTMLElement, bridge: ProbeBridge): void`, with the types in `src/core/bridge.ts`. `root` is `div#ui`, which lies over the canvas inside `#app`. The interface reads the app only through `bridge.state()` and `bridge.subscribe`, and acts only through the bridge's calls. It never touches the canvas.
- The bar: a stranger uses the globe without a word of instruction, and the picture stays the hero. This piece is judged as a social media showcase: no header, no tagline, no hint text, no labels beyond what is named here, nothing that looks like a web demo's control panel.
- What exists, and nothing else: one Shake control at the bottom edge (the single word from `src/copy.ts` in a thin quiet pill with a small glyph is allowed), four season marks as small glyphs of your own drawing (a snowflake, a blossom, a sun, a leaf, as inline SVG), the current one lit, each a button that changes season by shaking, and one sound control that shows its state. Accessible names come from `src/copy.ts` through visually hidden text and ARIA. Required attributes, read by the checks: `[data-control="shake"]`, `[data-control="season"][data-season="<id>"]` for each season in `config.seasons`, `[data-control="sound"]`, and `[data-menu]` on the menu.
- Behaviour: the controls dim when the visitor is idle and come back on movement (`bridge.activity()` is yours to call on pointer and key input). One soft pulse on the Shake control after `live.hintAfterSeconds` if nobody has shaken yet, once, never a loop. While `state.busy` the Shake control shows it and does not fire twice. While `!state.ready` nothing is shown that looks like a spinner over a blank page. The sound control is hidden when `!state.soundAvailable`. Keyboard: Space or Enter shakes, the arrow keys step the season marks, `S` toggles sound, every control has a visible focus state. `prefers-reduced-motion` and `state.reducedMotion` turn the pulse and the transitions off.
- The `M` key opens a small menu for the owner, hidden from the tab order: quality level (and auto), season, state (`landed`, `rest`, `settled`), diagnostic view (the list in `kit/04-LOOK.md` section 9 plus `none`), and play (`live`, `film-b`, `film-a`, `loop`).
- Three sizes: `formats.hd`, `formats.phone` and `formats.tablet`. On a phone the controls stay inside the safe areas and within thumb reach, and nothing overlaps the globe more than it must.
- CSS Modules. GSAP is available for easing. No UI framework. Surfaces are never pure black or pure white: a dark warm glass look that sits on a dark picture, thin, quiet, exact. Type: a self-hosted OFL font in `public/fonts/` with its row for `ASSETS.md`, or the system stack. No em dashes or exclamation points in any copy.
- The probe page as "The probe" describes it: runs through `bridge.run`, shows the table of results, the graphics card's name, the screen and the pick, then one block of plain text with a Copy button. Works on a phone.
- `tools/ui-preview/`: a small page (`npm run dev`, then `/tools/ui-preview/`) that mounts the interface on a mock bridge over one of the approved images as a backdrop, so you can look at it without the engine.
