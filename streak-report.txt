1. WHAT IS MADE, AND WHERE IT DIFFERS FROM MY WORDS OF 21:58
It is not a card stretched along its path with a tent of weights. Reading the petals' shader I found that a stretched card cannot hold a petal seen edge on, nor its light and its soft outline, and that fall's leaves are one model and not cards. So the same end is reached another way, and it sits in the shared gust (STAGE_GUST_GLSL in src/core/materials.ts), so that spring's petals and fall's leaves both have it from the same lines:
- Each sample's share of the shutter is its rank in time, even shares by rank, taken as leaveOut takes it in src/shake/change.ts (hold + rank / of * SHUTTER_CHANGE, one of-th long).
- That share is cut into 8 equal parts. The scatter is drawn once at the middle of each part, so each part follows the piece's own path and pace (the gust's own formula with a later time, nothing made up beside it).
- Every pixel keeps one of the 8, by its level in the still tile (src/core/still.ts), turned on by 0.618 of a whole with every sample. Neighbours keep unlike parts, and one pixel goes round all of them over the frame.
- So a pixel sees the scatter at as many moments as the frame has samples, one inside every sample's share, where before all pixels saw the same 76 moments. The row of copies becomes one smear. What is left is a fine grain, pixel to pixel, of the size of one sample's weight.
- The 8 parts are a second mesh over the same vertices (gustTwin), made the first time a film frame asks for it, shown in place of the first mesh for those samples alone.
It is inside the dressing's flight only: gustStreaks() says yes only for a sample that has a hold (a film frame), 8 samples or more (GUST_STREAK.join, the same count as LEAVE_OUT.join), and a shutter that is open between the first piece lifting and the last piece gone (or the first coming in and the last down): GUST_FLIGHT. The slabs ask only while two seasons are on stage and one of them is theirs.

13. ADDED 02:35 UTC, 28 November: THE THIRD MEND (c), EACH PART OF A SAMPLE SEEN FROM ITS OWN POINT OF THE LENS. THE RIBS GO, A FINER GRAIN STANDS IN THEIR PLACE
WHAT IT IS. A sample is seen from one point of the lens (src/scene/cameras.ts, `applyPose`). A petal out of focus is drawn at its place along its flight plus its blur disc times that point, so the eight parts of a sample lay together as a bunch and the bunches of a frame stood unevenly along the streak: ribs across the flight. src/film/motion.ts (`lensOrder`) names the same thing for the ball ("beads along a streak"). The mend shifts each part of the second mesh to a lens point of its own: the sample's point turned an eighth of the round a part and set on another ring of the lens (`gustLensPoint` in src/core/materials.ts, `stageGustSeen` in the vertex shader, three lines in each of the two slabs). The first mesh, the live page, the stills and every frame with nothing in the air are untouched.
