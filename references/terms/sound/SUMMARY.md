# ElevenLabs terms for sound effects and music: summary

Read live on 2026-11-24 UTC. Plan: Creator, through the API. The contract pages all carry the date 09 October 2026. Exact quotes and every page date are in `QUOTES.md` in this folder.

## The three answers

A. Sound effects inside a film and inside an app: allowed on a paid plan. The Sound Effects terms let you use output "including by incorporating it into films, trailers, advertising, video, games, applications, and other productions", but "only in respect of Output combined with other material". Mark: `stated`. URL: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms

B. Sound effects kept as separate audio files in a public repository: not allowed. The same clause says you may not "make Output available as isolated audio files or samples, or as part of any sound, sample, effects, or music library or other collection of sounds", unless it is through ElevenLabs Community Sharing or "any other use ElevenLabs expressly permits in writing". The terms do not name repositories, but a public repository of raw sound files is isolated files made available. Mark: `stated`. URL: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms

C. Music in a video posted by a company on social media: not clearly allowed on Creator, for two reasons. First, the Creator media rights are "All online and offline commercial use permitted, except film, TV, radio, and Studio Games", only the "Enterprise Music" plan has no exception, and "film" is not defined anywhere, so whether a social media video counts as film is not settled by the text. Second, the same table marks Creator "For Individual Use Only"; entities appear only from Scale up ("fewer than 10 employees"), and enrolling in a plan you are not eligible for "may result in suspension or termination of your account or loss of Output, Fees, or credits". Mark: `stated` for the film, TV, radio exception and for the individual use limit; `unknown` for whether a company social media video is "film" and for what "individual use" covers. URL: https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms

## Other points that matter for this use

All of these are `stated` unless marked otherwise. The quote for each is in `QUOTES.md`.

- Music files in a public repository carry the same risk as the sound files. The Music Terms forbid a "Music Library" on every plan below Enterprise, defined as "any collection of Output, in whole or in part, made available to third parties for those third parties to use, license, download, or incorporate into their content, whether or not for payment". Whether a code repository counts is `unknown`; the definition does not depend on payment. https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- The portfolio web app and music. The film, TV, radio exception also covers "Studio Games", defined as "a video game or other interactive or playable experience made available to the public" that is "commercialized by sale, advertising, or any other form of monetization". Whether a free public web app with no advertising counts is `unknown`. https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Open source licence on the repository. The Prohibited Use Policy forbids "Making our Services or Output available to your own end users or other third parties on terms more permissive than those that apply to you". A permissive licence that covers the audio files would do that. https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy
- Attribution. None is required for music on Creator ("No Attribution Required"). For sound effects the Help Center says attribution applies to the free plan only. https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms and https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform
- AI disclosure and labelling. You must "clearly disclose when people are interacting with AI where required by law or where failing to do so would materially mislead them". Following each social platform's rules on labelling AI content is your duty, and any marking ElevenLabs adds does not do it for you. https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy and https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Watermarks. You must not "remove, alter, or conceal ElevenLabs watermarks, metadata or other provenance signals for the purpose of disguising AI-generated content or evading detection". ElevenLabs says it embeds watermarks in generated audio. https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy
- Training. Output may not be used to train AI models or placed in training datasets. https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy
- Reselling. Sound effects may not be sold or licensed "on a standalone basis". Access to the music model may not be resold on Creator ("Model Resale" is "Prohibited"). https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms and https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Music streaming release. Releasing the music on streaming services is "Permitted" on Creator. This is a separate row from media rights and does not lift the film, TV, radio exception. https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Ownership. "you retain all rights in and to your Output", and you give ElevenLabs a licence that includes use "to improve the Services". Training on your data can be switched off in the "Data use" menu. https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use
- ElevenLabs name and logo. Using them needs written permission, so the video and app should not show ElevenLabs branding. https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use
- API key. The ElevenAPI Terms say you shall not "commit or distribute any credential or secret". The key must not be in the public repository. https://elevenlabs.io/elevenapi-terms
- Upgrading. The Music Terms say a move to a higher plan applies that plan's rights to music already made. Scale is the lowest plan that names entities, and only Enterprise Music has no film, TV, radio exception. https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Pages that say more than the contract. The music documentation says music is cleared "from film and television to podcasts and social media videos", and the sound effects product page names "YouTube videos, social media content, and even advertising". For sound effects this matches the terms. For music it conflicts with the plan table, and the terms are the contract. https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music and https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects
- Models are being retired. The documentation says `music_v1` will be removed on January 31, 2027 and `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` on March 1, 2027. https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music and https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/sound-effects
- Content ID and registering works: `unknown`. No mention was found in the Terms of Service, ElevenCreative Terms, Music Terms, ElevenAPI Terms or Prohibited Use Policy (text search of each page, plus a site search).
- The terms can change. All five contract pages were updated on 09 October 2026, so they should be read again before publishing.
