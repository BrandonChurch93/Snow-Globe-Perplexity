## Where pages disagree

Both sides are quoted above. These notes only point to them.

- Film and television for music. The documentation page (https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music) says music is cleared "from film and television to podcasts and social media videos" and then points to the Music Terms. The Music Terms table (https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms) excludes "film, TV, radio, and Studio Games" on every plan except Enterprise Music. The product pages https://elevenlabs.io/music and https://elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-api agree with the Music Terms on film and TV.
- Radio. The Music Terms table lists radio in the exception. The FAQ on https://elevenlabs.io/music lists only film, TV and Studio Games. The FAQ on https://elevenlabs.io/music/commercial speaks of "TV and radio distribution" and does not name film.
- Games. The Music Terms use the defined term "Studio Games". The page https://elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-api says "large studio game rights".
- Who may use the Creator plan for music. The Music Terms table says "For Individual Use Only" for Free, Starter, Creator and Pro. No pricing page, product page or Help Center article that was read repeats this limit. The sign-up page https://join.elevenlabs.io/music says tracks are cleared for "ads, games, and client work" with no limit named.
- Streaming on Starter. https://elevenlabs.io/music/commercial describes paid plans as "excluding streaming"; the Music Terms table prohibits Music Streaming Distribution on Free and Starter and permits it from Creator up.
- Old versus new music wording. The archived v1 terms (2025) limited the film, TV and advertisement exception to "offline" use. The current table has no such word.
