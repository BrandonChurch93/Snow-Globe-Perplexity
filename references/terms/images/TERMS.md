Date read: 2026-11-24 UTC. All pages were opened on that date. Some were served from the fetch cache, so each page's own date is given below.

Scope of the question: pictures (textures, material sheets) made through the image tool inside Perplexity Computer, then used by a company in (a) a showcase video posted on social media and (b) a public portfolio web app.

How to read this file:
- `stated` means the point is in a quote kept below, with its URL.
- `unknown` means no page I could open says it.
- Lines that start with "Reading:" are my own summary of the quotes. They are not quotes.
- Verdicts use only the quotes. This is a record of what the terms say, not legal advice.

> "BytePlus does not represent or warrant that any Output will be original, unique, protectable, registrable, or non-infringing. You are responsible for determining whether Output is suitable for your intended use."

Reading: "you" in these terms is BytePlus's customer. For a call made through Perplexity that customer is Perplexity, not the Perplexity user. What passes on to the user is set by Perplexity's terms in section 4 below.

### Commercial use (BytePlus route)

> "the claim is related to a trademark-related right as a result of your and/or your Authorized Users' use of such Output in trade or commerce;"

Reading: the terms expect Output to be used in trade, and they leave trademark risk with the user.

`stated`, General Terms for AI Services, sections 1.4 and 1.5:

> "You must comply with applicable law when using, publishing, or distributing any Generated Content, including any applicable labeling or disclosure requirements for AI-generated content."

> "If you make AI features or Generated Content available to end users, you are responsible for implementing reasonable safeguards appropriate to your use case, including user notices, safety controls, and human review where appropriate."

> "use, reference, copy, or register the trademarks, trade names, names, or logos of BytePlus or its affiliates (including but not limited to "Byteplus", "Seedance" and their respective version series, etc.) in any manner without the written consent of BytePlus, or state or imply that your products or services have been approved or endorsed by BytePlus;"

`stated`, Acceptable Use Policy, Responsible AI Requirements. You shall:

> "provide appropriate notices that users are interacting with an AI and that content is AI generated;"

`stated`, C2PA guide. Provenance data is added by default to Seedream 5.0 pro output:

> "BytePlus ModelArk embeds C2PA Content Credentials by default for currently supported image and video generation model outputs."

The supported model table on that page lists "Dola-Seedream-5.0-pro". The same page says: "Screenshots, transcoding, compression, or processing by others may remove or invalidate the Content Credential."

`stated`, Seedream tutorial: the request examples carry a `"watermark": true` parameter. Whether Perplexity sets it is `unknown`.

`stated`, Service Specific Terms, Model Services, section 2(1):

> "The BytePlus Model Services are not available in the United States."

Reading: this is a limit on where the service may be used, by the customer and its end users. It does not speak about using a finished picture. How it applies to a user in the United States who reaches Seedream through Perplexity is `unknown`.

`stated`, indemnity, Service Specific Terms, Model Services, section 3.1:

> "BytePlus shall defend you against a third party claim alleging that your permitted use of any Output generated by a BytePlus Model Services, which is NOT using third party models, BytePlus Modified Models or Third-party Modified Model, infringes the intellectual property rights of that third party"
