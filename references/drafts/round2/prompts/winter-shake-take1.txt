THE VORTEX. The whole body of liquid turns round the vertical axis of the globe, and the particles show it: they are drawn as motion-blurred streaks, each one a short curved dash smeared along its path. All the streaks follow one coherent rotation. In the front half of the globe they sweep from left to right in long shallow arcs that bend with the curve of the glass; behind the miniature they run back the other way, fainter. The swirl is a rising spiral: it lifts off the ground of the miniature at the lower left, winds up and round the cottage and the tree, tightens toward the top of the dome and curls over there, like a small tornado seen from the side. Streaks close against the front glass are large, long, soft and out of focus; streaks at the distance of the cottage and the tree are fine and crisp-edged; behind them the far side of the swirl is a pale, milky, turning haze. The storm fills the globe completely, from the front glass to the back glass and from the ground to the top of the dome, with no calm empty patch anywhere in the liquid.
