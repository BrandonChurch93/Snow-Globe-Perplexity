THE SEEDS AND FIREFLIES IN THE LIQUID. Keep their number, their positions and their three sizes as in image 1: about two hundred and fifty dandelion seeds and three hundred fireflies, filling the liquid from the front glass to the back glass and from the grass to the very top of the dome, with no empty patches. Do not thin them out and do not remove any. Render every dandelion seed as a real one hanging in clear liquid: a tiny brown seed under a fine white parachute of radiating silky filaments, each tilted its own way, the filaments catching the low golden sun. Render every firefly as a small point of yellow-green light with a soft halo, brightest in the shaded parts of the globe. The largest seeds and fireflies, close against the front glass, are big, soft and out of focus: pale feathery discs and round yellow-green bokeh. The mid-sized ones, at the distance of the cottage and the tree, are crisp. The smallest, behind the tree and the chimney, are a fine faint haze of pale specks and pinprick lights. Seeds and fireflies cross in front of the roof, the tree's crown and trunk, the lanterns and the pond, so the miniature is seen through them and stays clearly readable. Nothing is streaked or motion-blurred. Every seed and firefly is inside the glass; the air outside the glass is completely empty.

THE BASE. The same warm sandstone drum with its wind-carved layers, the same size and shape, with sand drifting off its ledge on the right as a thin falling veil. Set into the stone, flush with it, are more seashells than in image 1: scallops, small whelks, cockles and a spiral top shell, a dozen or more, ivory and sand-pink, each a real shell with fine ribs. Between them, pieces of real sea glass: frosted, matt, cloudy, never shiny and never round like beads, irregular tumbled shards with softened corners, in pale aqua, bottle green, seafoam and one amber piece, half sunk into the stone. The pale angular patches in image 1 show where some of them sit. No glossy droplets and no glass marbles on the base. No driftwood anywhere.

Nowhere on the glass is there a reflection of a window, a window frame or a room; only the sun flare and soft curved highlights.
