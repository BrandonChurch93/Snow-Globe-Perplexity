BASE. Carved from warm honey-coloured sandstone, wind-worn and sea-worn, with soft ripple layers in the stone. Set into it like a jeweller's inlay are real seashells, a fan scallop, cockles, a spiral whelk and tiny periwinkles, and smooth frosted pieces of sea glass in aqua, bottle green, amber and pale blue that glow where the low sun passes through them. Dry pale sand lies in the hollows and along the ledge, and drifts off the edge of the ledge at the front right in a thin falling veil of grains, piling into a small cone on the polished surface. The glass sits in a rim of smooth sandstone. No driftwood, no wood of any kind, no lettering.

GLASS. Perfectly clean and clear: no frost, no drops. The low sun, far behind the globe at the upper right, strikes the dome: a strong hot sun flare glints on the upper right of the glass, with a soft veiling glare and a faint warm lens-flare streak. The liquid-filled sphere works as a burning lens and focuses the light into bright warm caustics, curved rippling lines and one hot spot of golden light, on the black polished surface in front of and to the left of the base.

INSIDE. High summer at dusk. The big tree is in full deep green leaf, a dense, heavy, dark green crown. Tall sunflowers with big golden heads stand along the fence and beside the cottage wall, taller than the fence. Paper lanterns: a swag of round ribbed paper lanterns in red, orange, amber and cream hangs on a cord from the cottage eave across to the lowest branch of the tree, and more hang among the tree's branches; each is plainly a paper lantern, about as wide as the cottage door, lit from inside by a small flame and glowing, the brightest things in the scene. Thick summer grass with daisies; climbing roses on the cottage wall; the cottage windows softly lit; the lamppost lit, dimmer than the lanterns. The pond is perfectly still, a mirror reflecting the gold and violet dusk sky, the lanterns and the tree. No string lights along the eaves. No smoke.

PARTICLES. Fireflies and dandelion seeds, far denser than in image 2, filling the liquid at every depth from the front glass to the back glass and from the grass to the top of the dome. Fireflies: eighty or more tiny points of warm yellow-green light with soft halos; the nearest are large soft glowing discs close against the front glass, the middle ones small and bright, the farthest pinpricks behind the tree. Dandelion seeds: two hundred or more, each a fine stalk under a feathery white parachute of filaments, drifting at every depth, backlit so the filaments glow gold; the nearest large and out of focus against the front glass, the middle ones crisp, the farthest tiny. The cottage, tree, pond, lamppost, fence and path stay clearly readable through them. Every firefly and every seed is inside the glass; nothing floats in the air outside it.

ON THE SURFACE. A scatter of sand. Fine pale dry sand is scattered on the black polished surface around the foot of the base, thickest at the foot and thinning to single sparkling grains further out, with the small cone under the falling veil. The warm caustics play across the sand and the gloss. Nothing else lies on the surface.

The overall exposure is dusk-dark, so the lanterns, the fireflies, the lit windows and the lamppost visibly glow and spill warm light through the glass onto the sandstone and the surface.
