out from under it: the end was a level edge along the table (item 275).

What is there now. One number, imageFront, ends the zone.
- At rest (the foot down and upright, no leaving) it is the same step(0.0, xz.y), the same arithmetic in the same order.
- With the foot lifted or leaning (uFoot.w > 0.0 || dot(uLean.zw, uLean.zw) > 0.0, the very test by which the table's
  shade knows the foot is off the table, slabShade in src/scene/surface.ts) or the frost on its way (uGone.x > 0.0) it is
  smoothstep(-imageSpread, imageSpread, xz.y), with imageSpread = max(imageHalf, min(uGone.x, 6.0)).
- imageHalf is half the foot's chord at that place across the foot: the depth the foot hid there, either way from the
  axis. It is 58 mm in the middle of the foot, 0 at its two ends and beside it. So the end rises from the foot's back to
  its front, half way at the axis, and beside the foot, where the table is seen while the foot stands, the spread is 0
  and the step is drawn as before.
- min(uGone.x, 6.0) (IMAGE_LEAVE_MM = 6) is for the leaving alone: uGone.x is how far out the frost is, in mm, and the
  frost drawn at a place is the frost that lay that much nearer the axis, so the zone and its end travel out with it. The
  strip beside the foot would carry its step out over the table. So in the leaving the end is spread over the way the
  frost has gone, up to 6 mm either way: nothing at the leaving's first instant (no step in time), 6 mm from the moment
  the frost is 6 mm out. By change.ts and the config (60 frames a second, a shutter of half a frame) the frost leaves from
  film frame 666.72 to 672.72 and is out by 170 mm times the square of the share of that span: 0.4 to 2.9 mm while the
  shutter of 667 is open, 7.7 to 15.0 mm in 668, 24.6 to 36.5 mm in 669.

What it costs. At rest one comparison of three numbers that are 0. With the foot up or the frost leaving, one smoothstep,
a max and a min.

and the frost lies ..."): it holds the lines of the sheet (one step(0.0, xz.y) left, the branch, the spread, the zone's
